# Story arcs and themes

Arc tags from the curated data files (assigned from each episode's transcript), in four groups: recurring adversaries and factions, storytelling devices, arcs centred on one character, and broader themes. Episodes are in broadcast order within each arc.

## Adversaries and factions

### Q

8 episodes.

- S01E01-E02 [Encounter at Farpoint](episodes/101.md): Picard, new captain of the Enterprise, is confronted by Q, who judges humanity a savage race and sets Farpoint Station as a test. Picard and Riker discover the station is a captive lifeform, held by the Bandi, and free it, passing Q's test.
- S01E10 [Hide & Q](episodes/111.md): Q gives Riker the powers of the Q, hoping to lure him into the Continuum; Riker is tempted, but when he offers his friends their dearest wishes they all turn them down, and Q loses his wager with Picard.
- S02E16 [Q Who?](episodes/142.md): After Picard refuses Q's request to join the crew, Q throws the Enterprise seven thousand light years away, where a Borg cube attacks and kills eighteen crew. With the ship helpless, Picard admits 'I need you!' and Q sends them back.
- S03E13 [Déjà Q](episodes/161.md): Stripped of his powers, Q asks Picard for sanctuary on the Enterprise while the crew tries to push a falling moon away from Bre'el Four. Hunted by the Calamarain, Q shows some selflessness, is restored to the Continuum, and the moon returns to orbit.
- S04E20 [Qpid](episodes/194.md): Q, saying he is repaying a debt, throws Picard and his officers into a Robin Hood story to prove that loving Vash makes him weak. Picard rescues Vash as Maid Marian. Afterward she leaves with Q as his new partner.
- S06E06 [True Q](episodes/232.md): Intern Amanda Rogers, adopted by humans, is revealed by Q to be a Q herself. Q is sent to test her and teach her; he warns she may be killed unless she can refrain from using her power. She saves Tagra Four's people and chooses to join the Continuum.
- S06E15 [Tapestry](episodes/241.md): Dying after an attack, Picard is offered a second chance by Q to avoid the Nausicaan fight that cost him his heart. He does, but returns to a life as an unremarkable junior officer, and begs to go back and fight again.
- S07E25-E26 [All Good Things...](episodes/277.md): Q tells Picard, who is shifting between past, present and future, that humanity is condemned and that Picard causes its destruction. Picard finds that tachyon pulses from his ships in all three eras created the anti-time anomaly, and the three Enterprises collapse it. Q says the trial never ends.

### Borg

6 episodes.

- S02E16 [Q Who?](episodes/142.md): After Picard refuses Q's request to join the crew, Q throws the Enterprise seven thousand light years away, where a Borg cube attacks and kills eighteen crew. With the ship helpless, Picard admits 'I need you!' and Q sends them back.
- S03E26 [Best Of Both Worlds, part 1](episodes/174.md): The Borg invade Federation space and destroy a colony, Shelby arrives as a tactical expert and clashes with Riker, and after a costly battle Picard is captured and returns on screen as Locutus of Borg.
- S04E01 [Best of Both Worlds, part 2](episodes/175.md): Riker, promoted to captain, rescues Picard from the Borg; Data uses the link to Locutus, with Picard's help, to put the Borg to sleep after they have wrecked the fleet at Wolf 359.
- S05E23 [I, Borg](episodes/223.md): A lone injured Borg, named Hugh by Geordi, develops individuality; Picard abandons a plan to use him to destroy the Collective, and Hugh chooses to return to the Borg.
- S06E26 [Descent, part 1](episodes/252.md): Borg who act as individuals attack an outpost and the Enterprise; Data feels anger and, goaded by a captured Borg, goes off in a shuttle with him. Picard's team follows and finds a Borg hall, where Lore appears and Data declares the sons of Soong will destroy the Federation.
- S07E01 [Descent, part 2](episodes/253.md): Lore controls Data and experiments on captured Enterprise officers while Crusher commands the ship, rescuing the stranded crew and destroying a Borg ship. Picard's team restores Data's ethical programme, Hugh turns on Lore, and Data shoots Lore to deactivate him.

### Klingon

15 episodes.

- S01E20 [Heart Of Glory](episodes/120.md): Rescued from a dying freighter, three renegade Klingons urge Worf to join them in reclaiming the old warrior ways; when their escape endangers the Enterprise, Worf kills Korris and chooses Starfleet duty.
- S02E08 [A Matter of Honour](episodes/134.md): Riker volunteers for an exchange posting as first officer of the Klingon ship Pagh. When a hull-eating organism appears, Captain Kargan blames the Enterprise and prepares to attack. Riker keeps his oaths, has Kargan beamed away, takes command briefly, then hands it back.
- S02E20 [The Emissary](episodes/146.md): Federation emissary K'Ehleyr, whom Worf knew six years earlier, joins the Enterprise to stop a Klingon ship whose crew, asleep for seventy-five years, will attack Federation colonies believing the war continues. Worf's bluff as commander forces Captain K'Temoc to yield. K'Ehleyr and Worf part with feelings unresolved.
- S03E15 [Yesterday's Enterprise](episodes/163.md): The Enterprise-C emerges from a temporal rift, creating a timeline where the Federation is losing a war with the Klingons and Tasha Yar is alive; Guinan senses the wrongness, and Picard sends the Enterprise-C back to its fate with Tasha aboard.
- S03E17 [Sins Of The Father](episodes/165.md): Kurn, a Klingon exchange officer, tells Worf the High Council has branded their father Mogh a traitor. Worf and Picard, as cha'DIch, learn the Council knew Mogh was innocent, so Worf accepts discommendation to spare Kurn and prevent civil war.
- S04E07 [Reunion](episodes/181.md): Dying K'mpec asks Picard to arbitrate the Klingon succession and find his poisoner. K'Ehleyr, who has arrived with Worf's son Alexander, investigates Duras over Khitomer and is killed; she names Duras, and Worf kills him despite Riker's arrival.
- S04E24 [The Mind's Eye](episodes/198.md): Geordi is abducted and brainwashed by the Romulans, who control him through his VISOR to assassinate Klingon Governor Vagh and wreck the Federation-Klingon alliance; Ambassador Kell is their agent, and an unnamed woman commander is heard but not seen.
- S04E26 [Redemption](episodes/200.md): Gowron's succession is challenged by Toral, son of Duras, and his aunts Lursa and B'Etor, who have Romulan allies; Picard refuses to take sides, and Worf resigns from Starfleet to serve Gowron, who restores his honour.
- S05E01 [Redemption part 2](episodes/201.md): Picard sets a tachyon detection blockade on the Romulan-Klingon border to expose Romulan help for the Duras. Data, commanding the Sutherland, disobeys orders and fires on the hidden convoy, so Sela withdraws. Gowron wins, and Worf spares the captured Toral.
- S05E07 [Unification, part 1](episodes/208.md): Sent to find Ambassador Spock, reported on Romulus, Picard visits the dying Sarek, then he and Data, disguised as Romulans, travel on a borrowed Klingon ship. Sarek dies on the way. On Romulus, Pardek's soldiers take them off the street, and Spock reveals himself.
- S06E13 [Aquiel](episodes/239.md): Geordi studies the logs of missing Lieutenant Aquiel Uhnari and falls for her when Klingons return her alive. Crusher finds the remains on the relay station are a coalescent organism that copies what it absorbs. Aquiel and Morag are held for observation, and Geordi destroys the creature when it copies his dog.
- S06E17 [Birthright, part 2](episodes/243.md): Held by Tokath at a camp where Klingon captives from Khitomer live with Romulans, Worf teaches the young people Klingon ways. Tokath orders his execution, but Toq, Ba'el and the elders stand with him and the young leave with Worf. He tells Picard no camp existed.
- S06E20 [The Chase](episodes/246.md): After Professor Galen, Picard's old archaeology teacher, is killed, Picard pursues his number blocks, which prove to be DNA fragments forming a program. Cardassians, Klingons and Romulans race him for the last piece. It plays a recorded message from an ancient being who seeded life on many worlds.
- S06E23 [Rightful Heir](episodes/249.md): Worf, in a crisis of faith, goes to Boreth, where Klingon Guardians present a returned Kahless. Gowron calls him an impostor, and Worf learns he is a clone. To avoid civil war, Worf proposes Kahless become Emperor as a figurehead, and Gowron accepts.
- S07E21 [Firstborn](episodes/273.md): K'mtar, who says he was sent to protect Worf from assassins, pushes Worf's son Alexander toward the warrior's path and urges Worf to send him to a Klingon school. He turns out to be Alexander from forty years in the future, who staged the attack; Worf talks him into accepting himself.

### Romulan

19 episodes.

- S01E26 [The Neutral Zone](episodes/126.md): The Enterprise revives three humans frozen since the 20th century and makes first contact in decades with the Romulans, who say they have also lost outposts to an unknown enemy; Federation outposts have been destroyed too.
- S02E11 [Contagion](episodes/137.md): After the Yamato destroys itself, an alien Iconian programme carried in her downloaded log cripples the Enterprise and a Romulan ship. Picard destroys the Iconian control room, Data's memory wipe suggests Geordi's fix, and the Enterprise shuts down and reloads clean.
- S03E07 [The Enemy](episodes/155.md): Geordi is stranded on storm-wracked Galorndon Core with a Romulan and they must cooperate to survive; Worf refuses to give the ribosomes a dying Romulan needs, and the Enterprise faces down a Romulan warbird at the border.
- S03E10 [The Defector](episodes/158.md): A Romulan who first calls himself Setal, then admits he is Admiral Jarok, defects to the Enterprise warning of a secret Romulan base at Nelvana Three. Picard finds no base, only a trap that Tomalak springs; Klingon ships force the Romulans back, and Jarok kills himself.
- S03E20 [Tin Man](episodes/168.md): The Betazoid telepath Tam Elbrun makes contact with Gomtuu, a lonely living ship, while the Romulans race to seize or destroy it; Gomtuu destroys one warbird, and Tam chooses to stay with it.
- S04E11 [Data's Day](episodes/185.md): Data narrates a day in his life to Commander Maddox: he learns to dance, his friendly meddling nearly derails the wedding of O'Brien and Keiko, and he helps uncover a Romulan spy posing as a Vulcan ambassador.
- S04E24 [The Mind's Eye](episodes/198.md): Geordi is abducted and brainwashed by the Romulans, who control him through his VISOR to assassinate Klingon Governor Vagh and wreck the Federation-Klingon alliance; Ambassador Kell is their agent, and an unnamed woman commander is heard but not seen.

Data, commanding the Sutherland, disobeys orders and fires on the hidden convoy, so Sela withdraws. Gowron wins, and Worf spares the captured Toral.
- S05E07 [Unification, part 1](episodes/208.md): Sent to find Ambassador Spock, reported on Romulus, Picard visits the dying Sarek, then he and Data, disguised as Romulans, travel on a borrowed Klingon ship. Sarek dies on the way. On Romulus, Pardek's soldiers take them off the street, and Spock reveals himself.
- S05E08 [Unification, part 2](episodes/207.md): Picard and Spock find that Proconsul Neral's reunification offer is a plot by Sela to send an invasion force to Vulcan in stolen Vulcan ships. They escape her, and Spock's warning reaches the Enterprise, which stops the ships. Spock stays on Romulus to help the underground movement.
- S05E24 [The Next Phase](episodes/224.md): After a transporter accident, La Forge and Ro are phased by a Romulan experimental device and believed dead. Unseen, they attend their own memorial party and uncover the Romulans' plan to wreck the Enterprise's warp engines. Data's anyon beam makes them visible again.
- S06E14 [Face of the Enemy](episodes/240.md): Troi wakes surgically altered as Tal Shiar Major Rakal aboard a Romulan warbird. Subcommander N'Vek, a dissident, needs her to deliver defecting Vice Proconsul M'ret to the Federation. She bluffs Commander Toreth, then tricks the Enterprise into lowering shields and transports M'ret across. N'Vek dies and Troi is rescued.
- S06E16 [Birthright, part 1](episodes/242.md): Worf is told by the Yridian Jaglom Shrek that his father Mogh survived Khitomer in a Romulan prison camp, and goes there. Meanwhile a plasma shock gives Data visions of Doctor Soong, and he activates dormant circuits that let him dream. Worf is seized in the camp.

- S06E25 [Timescape](episodes/251.md): Returning from a conference, Picard, Troi, Data and Geordi find the Enterprise and a Romulan warbird frozen in fragmented time, with a warp core breach slowly expanding. They learn aliens nesting in the Romulan engine core caused it, reverse time, and Picard breaks the power transfer, saving the ship.
- S07E04 [Gambit, part 1](episodes/256.md): Believing Picard was killed, Riker investigates and is captured by Baran's mercenaries, who are looting Romulan artefacts. He finds Picard alive, undercover as "Galen", and agrees to play a disgraced officer to win Baran's trust. Riker shuts down the Enterprise's shields, and the mercenaries fire on it.
- S07E12 [The Pegasus](episodes/264.md): Admiral Pressman brings the Enterprise to find the lost Pegasus before the Romulans do, secretly intending to restart its illegal phasing cloak experiment. Riker, who once backed him in a mutiny aboard the Pegasus, refuses, and Picard arrests Pressman for violating the Treaty of Algeron.

### Cardassian

8 episodes.

- S04E12 [The Wounded](episodes/186.md): Captain Maxwell of the Phoenix, who lost his family in a Cardassian raid, destroys Cardassian ships, claiming they are rearming. Picard, with Gul Macet aboard, tracks him down and, with O'Brien's help, persuades him to surrender, preserving the peace and warning Macet that the Federation is watching.
- S05E03 [Ensign Ro](episodes/203.md): Picard is sent to find Bajoran terrorist leader Orta, aided by newly assigned, disgraced Ensign Ro. Ro admits Admiral Kennelly secretly meant her to offer Orta weapons. Picard exposes the Solarion Four attack as likely a Cardassian setup, and persuades Ro to stay in Starfleet.
- S06E10 [Chain of Command, part 1](episodes/236.md): Vice Admiral Nechayev replaces Picard with Captain Jellico to negotiate with the Cardassians, while Picard, Worf and Crusher secretly enter Cardassian space to find a suspected metagenic weapon lab. It is a trap, and Picard is captured and interrogated by Madred.
- S06E11 [Chain of Command, part 2](episodes/237.md): Gul Madred tortures the captured Picard for Starfleet's defence plans, demanding he say he sees five lights. Meanwhile Jellico, with Riker piloting, mines the Cardassian ships hidden in a nebula and forces them to withdraw and release Picard, who says there are four lights.
- S06E20 [The Chase](episodes/246.md): After Professor Galen, Picard's old archaeology teacher, is killed, Picard pursues his number blocks, which prove to be DNA fragments forming a program. Cardassians, Klingons and Romulans race him for the last piece. It plays a recorded message from an ancient being who seeded life on many worlds.
- S07E15 [Lower Decks](episodes/267.md): Four junior officers compete for a promotion to Ops while Picard secretly tests Ensign Sito for a mission; she poses as a prisoner to help a Cardassian informant cross the border and is reported killed.
- S07E20 [Journey's End](episodes/272.md): Wesley returns from the Academy troubled and resigns after a vision of his father; the Federation hands the colony world Dorvan V to Cardassia, its colonists choose to stay under Cardassian rule, and the Traveler reveals himself and offers to guide Wesley.
- S07E24 [Preemptive Strike](episodes/276.md): Starfleet sends Lieutenant Ro undercover into the Maquis, and Picard has her lure them into a trap at the Hugora Nebula. After Macias is killed in a Cardassian attack, Ro warns the Maquis, abandons Starfleet and leaves with them, apologising to Picard.

### Ferengi

8 episodes.

- S01E05 [The Last Outpost](episodes/107.md): The Enterprise pursues a Ferengi ship that stole a T9 energy converter, and both ships are held by a force field from a planet. An away team and Ferengi are tested by Portal, a Tkon guardian; Riker answers its challenge and both ships are freed.
- S01E09 [The Battle](episodes/110.md): The Ferengi DaiMon Bok gives Picard his old ship, the Stargazer, as a trap; a hidden thought-control device makes Picard relive the Battle of Maxia, where he destroyed the ship commanded by Bok's son, until Riker's crew breaks the control.
- S02E21 [Peak Performance](episodes/147.md): In a war game against Riker's stripped-down Hathaway, Picard's Enterprise is hit by a real Ferengi attack led by Bractor. Data, shaken after losing Strategema to Kolrami, helps Picard fake the Hathaway's destruction to scare the Ferengi off, then forces Kolrami to a stalemate.
- S03E08 [The Price](episodes/156.md): Barzan's Premier Bhavani takes bids for a stable wormhole aboard the Enterprise. Negotiator Devinoni Ral, who is part Betazoid, romances Troi and uses empathy to win for the Chrysalians, staging a Ferengi missile threat. Troi exposes him. Geordi and Data find the wormhole worthless.
- S03E24 [Ménage à Troi](episodes/172.md): DaiMon Tog of the Ferengi kidnaps Lwaxana Troi for her telepathy, along with Deanna and Riker. Riker hides a signal in the static, and Lwaxana offers to stay with Tog. Picard's staged jealous ultimatum gets her back. Wesley misses the Academy exam and is promoted to full Ensign.
- S06E07 [Rascals](episodes/233.md): Picard, Guinan, Ro and Keiko are turned into children when a molecular reversion field disrupts their beam-out. Renegade Ferengi under DaiMon Lurin then seize the Enterprise; the children use their size to disable the Ferengi, retake the ship, and are restored to adults.
- S06E22 [Suspicions](episodes/248.md): Crusher invites scientists to see Ferengi Doctor Reyga's metaphasic shield. After a pilot's death and Reyga's apparent suicide, she defies Picard's ban and autopsies Reyga, then flies the shuttle to prove the shield works, exposing the saboteur Jo'Bril and winning reinstatement.
- S07E22 [Bloodlines](episodes/274.md): Bok, stripped of his DaiMon rank, returns to avenge his son's death at Picard's hands; he threatens Picard's supposed son Jason Vigo, whose DNA he has faked, until Picard sees through it and confronts him on his ship.

## Devices and formats

### Data

36 episodes.

- S01E03 [The Naked Now](episodes/103.md): An alcohol-like contaminant from the dead research ship Tsiolkovsky spreads through the Enterprise, and Wesley seizes Engineering as a star collapses. Crusher adapts a cure from Kirk's Enterprise records, and Data's chip repair plus Wesley's repulser beam let the ship escape.
- S01E13 [Datalore](episodes/114.md): On Data's home planet, the Enterprise crew find a disassembled android, Lore, and rebuild him. Lore drugs Data, impersonates him and calls a crystalline entity to the ship to feed on its life. Data and Wesley stop him and beam him away, and the entity leaves.
- S01E24 [We'll Always Have Paris](episodes/124.md): Picard answers a distress call from scientist Paul Manheim, whose time experiments are distorting time across thousands of light years, and finds his wife Jenice, whom Picard once failed to meet in Paris. Data seals the breach, and Picard says goodbye to Jenice in a holodeck Paris.
- S02E03 [Elementary, Dear Data](episodes/129.md): Geordi asks the computer for an opponent capable of defeating Data, and Professor Moriarty becomes self-aware, takes over the holodeck and holds Pulaski. He wants to keep existing; Picard cannot free him, so Moriarty returns control and his programme is saved.
- S02E04 [The Outrageous Okona](episodes/130.md): Roguish freighter captain Okona is demanded by Debin of Atlec for dishonouring his daughter and by Kushell of Straleb for theft. Okona reveals he was only carrying messages between the real lovers, Yanar and Benzan, and the couple agree to marry. Meanwhile Data uses a holodeck comic to learn humour.
- S02E06 [The Schizoid Man](episodes/131.md): The dying scientist Ira Graves secretly transfers his mind into Data's body, wanting to live on with his assistant Kareen, and refuses to give it up until his behaviour turns violent; he then uploads himself into the ship's computer.
- S02E09 [The Measure of a Man](episodes/135.md): Commander Maddox wants to dismantle Data to learn how to build more androids; at a hearing with Riker prosecuting and Picard defending, Captain Louvois rules that Data has the right to choose, and he refuses the procedure.
- S02E11 [Contagion](episodes/137.md): After the Yamato destroys itself, an alien Iconian programme carried in her downloaded log cripples the Enterprise and a Romulan ship. Picard destroys the Iconian control room, Data's memory wipe suggests Geordi's fix, and the Enterprise shuts down and reloads clean.
- S02E15 [Pen Pals](episodes/141.md): Data answers a message from Sarjenka, a girl on Drema Four, and asks Picard to help when her planet starts tearing apart. After debate, Picard allows it. Wesley's team finds dilithium crystals are the cause, resonators stop them, and Sarjenka's memory of Data is wiped.
- S02E21 [Peak Performance](episodes/147.md): In a war game against Riker's stripped-down Hathaway, Picard's Enterprise is hit by a real Ferengi attack led by Bractor. Data, shaken after losing Strategema to Kolrami, helps Picard fake the Hathaway's destruction to scare the Ferengi off, then forces Kolrami to a stalemate.
- S03E02 [The Ensigns of Command](episodes/149.md): Data is sent to Tau Cygna Five to evacuate fifteen thousand human colonists before the Sheliak settle it. Gosheven refuses, so Data threatens to destroy the colony's aqueduct and wins them over, while Picard uses a treaty arbitration clause to win three weeks.
- S03E16 [The Offspring](episodes/164.md): Data builds a daughter, Lal, and raises her aboard the Enterprise. Admiral Haftel orders her moved to Galor Four, and Picard backs Data's refusal. Lal feels fear, suffers cascade failure and dies; Data transfers her memories into himself.
- S03E22 [The Most Toys](episodes/170.md): Collector Kivas Fajo fakes Data's death in a shuttle explosion and holds him captive, while La Forge doubts the accident. Fajo's assistant Varria helps Data escape and is killed. Data holds Fajo at disruptor point until the Enterprise beams him back; Fajo is arrested.
- S04E03 [Brothers](episodes/177.md): Data hijacks the Enterprise, answering a homing signal from his creator Dr Soong, who is dying and wants to give him an emotion chip. Lore, also summoned, impersonates Data, takes the chip and escapes, leaving Soong alive.
- S04E06 [Legacy](episodes/180.md): Hayne's Coalition sends Tasha Yar's sister Ishara to help the Enterprise rescue two freighter crewmen held by the Alliance on Turkana Four. After befriending Data, she tries to overload the Alliance's defences so Coalition troops can attack, and Data stops her.
- S04E11 [Data's Day](episodes/185.md): Data narrates a day in his life to Commander Maddox: he learns to dance, his friendly meddling nearly derails the wedding of O'Brien and Keiko, and he helps uncover a Romulan spy posing as a Vulcan ambassador.
- S04E13 [Devil's Due](episodes/187.md): Ardra claims the people of Ventax Two and the Enterprise under a thousand-year-old contract. Picard demands arbitration with Data as judge, and with Geordi and Riker finding her cloaked ship, he shows her powers are tricks. Ardra withdraws and the contract is dissolved.
- S04E14 [Clues](episodes/188.md): After a supposed wormhole, the Enterprise crew find clues that a day is missing while Data refuses to explain. The Paxans, who hide their system, erased everyone's memory; Picard ordered Data to keep silent. Picard persuades the Paxans to try again, and the crew forget once more.
- S04E25 [In Theory](episodes/199.md): Data, advised by crewmates, builds a programme to pursue a romance with Jenna D'Sora while the Enterprise is damaged by spatial deformations in the Mar Oscura nebula. Picard pilots a shuttle to guide the ship out. Jenna ends the relationship, saying Data cannot feel anything for her.
- S05E01 [Redemption part 2](episodes/201.md): Picard sets a tachyon detection blockade on the Romulan-Klingon border to expose Romulan help for the Duras. Data, commanding the Sutherland, disobeys orders and fires on the hidden convoy, so Sela withdraws. Gowron wins, and Worf spares the captured Toral.
- S05E04 [Silicon Avatar](episodes/204.md): Dr Kila Marr, whose son died on Omicron Theta, joins the Enterprise to hunt the Crystalline Entity that destroyed Melona colony. Picard wants to communicate with it, but when contact is made she keeps up a lethal graviton signal against his order and shatters it.
- S05E07 [Unification, part 1](episodes/208.md): Sent to find Ambassador Spock, reported on Romulus, Picard visits the dying Sarek, then he and Data, disguised as Romulans, travel on a borrowed Klingon ship. Sarek dies on the way. On Romulus, Pardek's soldiers take them off the street, and Spock reveals himself.
- S05E11 [Hero Worship](episodes/211.md): Timothy, orphaned when the research ship Vico is wrecked in a Black Cluster, pretends to be an android like Data. He blames himself for the disaster, but his memory of "more shields" lets Data realise shields amplify the wavefronts, saving the Enterprise. Timothy then drops the act.
- S05E26 [Time's Arrow, part 1](episodes/226.md): Data's head is found in a cave beneath San Francisco, dated five centuries old. While the Enterprise investigates aliens on Devidia, Data is pulled into 1893 San Francisco, where he finds Guinan and Clemens. Picard's away team follows the aliens through a portal.
- S06E08 [A Fistful of Datas](episodes/234.md): Geordi's experimental interface makes Data's personal programming replace a computer subroutine, so the Wild West holodeck programme's outlaws take Data's face and abilities. Worf and Troi, with the safeguards off, must rescue kidnapped Alexander by facing Frank Hollander in a showdown, then the programme ends.
- S06E09 [The Quality of Life](episodes/235.md): Data believes Doctor Farallon's exocomps may be alive and tries to stop her using them as labourers. - S04E20 [Qpid](episodes/194.md): Q, saying he is repaying a debt, throws Picard and his officers into a Robin Hood story to prove that loving Vash makes him weak. Picard rescues Vash as Maid Marian. Afterward she leaves with Q as his new partner.
- S05E20 [Cost of Living](episodes/220.md): Lwaxana Troi prepares to marry Minister Campio, whom she has never met, and befriends Alexander, undermining Worf's rules. Metal parasites eating the ship's nitrium nearly destroy it until Data lures them away with a nitrium beam. Lwaxana arrives at the wedding naked and Campio leaves.
- S06E07 [Rascals](episodes/233.md): Picard, Guinan, Ro and Keiko are turned into children when a molecular reversion field disrupts their beam-out. Renegade Ferengi under DaiMon Lurin then seize the Enterprise; the children use their size to disable the Ferengi, retake the ship, and are restored to adults.
- S06E08 [A Fistful of Datas](episodes/234.md): Geordi's experimental interface makes Data's personal programming replace a computer subroutine, so the Wild West holodeck programme's outlaws take Data's face and abilities. Worf and Troi, with the safeguards off, must rescue kidnapped Alexander by facing Frank Hollander in a showdown, then the programme ends.
- S07E02 [Liaisons](episodes/254.md): Iyaaran ambassadors visit the Enterprise to experience pleasure, antagonism and love. Troi hosts chocolate-loving Loquel and Worf endures the rude Byleth, while Picard, stranded on a planet, is courted by Anna, who turns out to be Voval in disguise.
