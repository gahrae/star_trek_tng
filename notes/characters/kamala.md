## Key facts

- Kriosian empathic metamorph bred to become whatever her partner desires, intended as a peace gift to Chancellor Alrik of Valt.
- Freed early from stasis by the Ferengi, she bonds with Picard rather than her intended husband.
- Goes through with the marriage anyway, carrying Picard's sense of duty with her (*The Perfect Mate*).
