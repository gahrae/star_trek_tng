## Synopsis

In nineteenth-century San Francisco the crew hunt aliens who drain human neural energy, with Samuel Clemens on their trail, while Picard meets the young Guinan and is stranded with her until Clemens helps him return.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Time's Arrow, part 1* (S05E26)](../episodes/226.md): Resolves the cliffhanger: Picard's team follows the aliens through their portal to nineteenth-century San Francisco.

## Log entries
