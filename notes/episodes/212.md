## Synopsis

Visiting Ullian telepaths are suspected when Troi, Riker and Crusher fall into comas after nightmarish memory intrusions. Troi's memory probe seems to point to Tarmin, but Data and La Forge's records show it was his son Jev, who is caught attacking Troi again.

## Log entries

> Captain's log, stardate 45429.3. While on a mapping survey, we are conveying a delegation of Ullians to Kaldra Four. These telepathic historians conduct their research by retrieving long-forgotten memories.

> Captain's log, stardate 45430.9. Counsellor Troi has fallen into a deep coma. After a thorough examination, Doctor Crusher has been unable to find signs of illness or infection.

> Medical log, stardate 45431.7. Commander Riker is the second officer who has fallen into an unexplained coma. I have examined the Ullians and ruled out the possibility that they carry a harmful organism.

> Captain's log, stardate 45433.2. We have set a course for Starbase four forty, where the Ullians will disembark and return home.

> Captain's log, stardate 45435.8. Doctor Crusher and Commander Riker regained consciousness while we were en route to the Ullian home world. The slow process of their recovery has begun.
