## Synopsis

Etana, a Ktarian, spreads an addictive game that controls the Enterprise crew. Wesley Crusher and Robin Lefler investigate, but Robin is taken too. Wesley is caught and forced to play, until Data, whom the crew had deactivated, frees everyone with an optical pulse.
