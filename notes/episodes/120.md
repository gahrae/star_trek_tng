## Synopsis

Rescued from a dying freighter, three renegade Klingons urge Worf to join them in reclaiming the old warrior ways; when their escape endangers the Enterprise, Worf kills Korris and chooses Starfleet duty.

## Log entries

> Captain's log, stardate 41503.7. We have entered the Neutral Zone, where a Talarian freighter has been severely damaged in a battle. I have sent an away team to investigate.
