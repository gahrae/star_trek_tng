## Synopsis

Troi investigates a suicide and, through an empathic imprint left by a murder eight years earlier, uncovers the killer; she and Worf become lovers.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Parallels* (S07E11)](../episodes/263.md): Related rather than explicit: Worf and Troi are married in one reality in Parallels and become lovers in Eye of the Beholder.
