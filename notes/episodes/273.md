## Synopsis

K'mtar, who says he was sent to protect Worf from assassins, pushes Worf's son Alexander toward the warrior's path and urges Worf to send him to a Klingon school. He turns out to be Alexander from forty years in the future, who staged the attack; Worf talks him into accepting himself.

## Continuity

- Follows [*New Ground* (S05E10)](../episodes/210.md): Worf's worry in New Ground that Alexander will not live as a Klingon returns when Alexander, as K'mtar, comes back from the future.
