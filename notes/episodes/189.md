## Synopsis

Riker, posing as Rivas Jakara, is hurt on Malcor III and held by frightened doctors and Minister Krola. Picard makes first contact with Chancellor Durken, who frees Riker but, finding his people unready, delays warp flight and asks the Enterprise to leave. Mirasta Yale goes with them.
