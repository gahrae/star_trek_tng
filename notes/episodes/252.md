## Synopsis

Borg who act as individuals attack an outpost and the Enterprise; Data feels anger and, goaded by a captured Borg, goes off in a shuttle with him. Picard's team follows and finds a Borg hall, where Lore appears and Data declares the sons of Soong will destroy the Federation.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Brothers* (S04E03)](../episodes/177.md): Lore, last seen in Brothers, reappears in Descent and stands with Data: 'The sons of Soong have joined together.'
- Follows [*I, Borg* (S05E23)](../episodes/223.md): Hugh, sent back to the Borg in I, Borg, may explain why the Borg act as individuals; Picard defends the decision.
- Leads to [*Descent, part 2* (S07E01)](../episodes/253.md): Resolves the cliffhanger: Lore controls Data until Picard's team restores his ethics and Hugh's Borg turn on Lore.
