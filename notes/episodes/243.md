## Synopsis

Held by Tokath at a camp where Klingon captives from Khitomer live with Romulans, Worf teaches the young people Klingon ways. Tokath orders his execution, but Toq, Ba'el and the elders stand with him and the young leave with Worf. He tells Picard no camp existed.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Birthright, part 1* (S06E16)](../episodes/242.md): Resolves the cliffhanger: Worf, seized at the camp Shrek described, finds it is no true prison camp.
