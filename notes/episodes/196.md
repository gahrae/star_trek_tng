## Synopsis

Lwaxana becomes close to Timicin, a Kaelon Two scientist expected to end his life at sixty under the Resolution. She urges him to refuse and he seeks asylum, but after his daughter's rebuke he returns home, and Lwaxana goes with him.

## Log entries
