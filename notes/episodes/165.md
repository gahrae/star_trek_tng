## Synopsis

Kurn, a Klingon exchange officer, tells Worf the High Council has branded their father Mogh a traitor. Worf and Picard, as cha'DIch, learn the Council knew Mogh was innocent, so Worf accepts discommendation to spare Kurn and prevent civil war.

## Continuity

- Leads to [*Reunion* (S04E07)](../episodes/181.md): Duras, whose father Ja'rod was the real traitor, brought the charge against Mogh in Sins of the Father; Worf kills him in Reunion, though Mogh's innocence stays untold.
- Leads to [*Redemption* (S04E26)](../episodes/200.md): Worf, discommended in Sins of the Father, has his honour restored by Gowron.
- Leads to [*Redemption part 2* (S05E01)](../episodes/201.md): Gowron finally admits that Duras' family wrongly took Worf's name and honour, as in Sins of the Father.
