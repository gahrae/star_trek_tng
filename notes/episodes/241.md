## Synopsis

Dying after an attack, Picard is offered a second chance by Q to avoid the Nausicaan fight that cost him his heart. He does, but returns to a life as an unremarkable junior officer, and begs to go back and fight again.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Samaritan Snare* (S02E17)](../episodes/143.md): Q shows Picard the bar fight that cost him his heart, mentioned in Samaritan Snare.
