## Synopsis

Crusher invites scientists to see Ferengi Doctor Reyga's metaphasic shield. After a pilot's death and Reyga's apparent suicide, she defies Picard's ban and autopsies Reyga, then flies the shuttle to prove the shield works, exposing the saboteur Jo'Bril and winning reinstatement.
