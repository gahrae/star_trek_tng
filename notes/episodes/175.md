## Synopsis

Riker, promoted to captain, rescues Picard from the Borg; Data uses the link to Locutus, with Picard's help, to put the Borg to sleep after they have wrecked the fleet at Wolf 359.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Best Of Both Worlds, part 1* (S03E26)](../episodes/174.md): Resolves the cliffhanger: Riker fires on the cube with Picard aboard as Locutus.
- Leads to [*Family* (S04E02)](../episodes/178.md): The Enterprise is under refit after the Borg incident and Picard recovers at home in France.

## Log entries

> Captain's Log, stardate 44002.3. Repairs are complete, and the Enterprise is warping to rendezvous with Starfleet at Wolf three five nine. Communications from the site of battle have been cut off, possibly by Borg interference.
