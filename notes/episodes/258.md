## Synopsis

Data's dream programme turns to nightmares and waking hallucinations, and he stabs Troi. Crusher finds interphasic organisms feeding on the crew; Data's dreams mirror them, and an interphasic pulse from his adjusted subprocessor eliminates them.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Birthright, part 1* (S06E16)](../episodes/242.md): Data's dream programme, activated in Birthright, turns to nightmares.
