## Synopsis

The Enterprise's systems form an intelligence that takes over the ship, with a holodeck train as its imagination, and builds an object in the cargo bay. Picard's crew cooperates, creating vertion particles with a modified torpedo, and the new lifeform leaves into space.

## Log entries

> Captain's log, supplemental. The Enterprise is back under our control. All traces of the emergent intelligence are gone and the object it created has disappeared into space.
