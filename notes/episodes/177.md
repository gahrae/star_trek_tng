## Synopsis

Data hijacks the Enterprise, answering a homing signal from his creator Dr Soong, who is dying and wants to give him an emotion chip. Lore, also summoned, impersonates Data, takes the chip and escapes, leaving Soong alive.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Datalore* (S01E13)](../episodes/114.md): Lore, sent into space at the end of Datalore, drifts for nearly two years before their creator Soong reactivates him.
- Leads to [*Descent, part 2* (S07E01)](../episodes/253.md): Lore uses the emotion chip he took in Brothers to control Data.
- Referenced by [*Descent, part 1* (S06E26)](../episodes/252.md): Lore, last seen in Brothers, reappears in Descent and stands with Data: 'The sons of Soong have joined together.'
- Referenced by [*Inheritance* (S07E10)](../episodes/262.md): Data and La Forge recall the emotion chip from Brothers when Juliana, who says she was Soong's wife, appears.
