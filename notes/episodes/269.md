## Synopsis

An ancient alien archive inside a comet reshapes the Enterprise into its own culture's artefacts and fills Data with personalities, including the queen Masaka. Picard finds the symbols for her temple and her counterpart Korgano, poses as Korgano, and puts her to sleep, restoring the ship and Data.
