## Synopsis

Admiral Jameson, summoned by Karnas to negotiate for hostages on Mordan Four, secretly overdoses on an alien de-aging drug. He admits his weapons deal began the planet's civil war. After a failed raid he surrenders to Karnas, dies as a young man, and the hostages are freed.
