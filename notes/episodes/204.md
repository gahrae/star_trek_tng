## Synopsis

Dr Kila Marr, whose son died on Omicron Theta, joins the Enterprise to hunt the Crystalline Entity that destroyed Melona colony. Picard wants to communicate with it, but when contact is made she keeps up a lethal graviton signal against his order and shatters it.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Datalore* (S01E13)](../episodes/114.md): The Crystalline Entity that Lore called to the Enterprise in Datalore is tracked and destroyed by Marr.
