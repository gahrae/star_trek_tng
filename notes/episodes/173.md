## Synopsis

Dr. Crusher saves an injured amnesiac who can heal others. Commander Sunad of Zalkon demands him back as an escaped criminal, since his people kill those who show these changes. Recovering his memory, John transforms into a being of energy, shows Sunad he is no threat, and departs.
