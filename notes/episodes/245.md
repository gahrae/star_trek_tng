## Synopsis

Captain Picard falls in love with Lieutenant Commander Nella Daren, sharing music with her and telling her about his flute. When she leads deflector teams protecting a colony evacuation and nearly dies, he decides he cannot endanger her again and they agree to part postings.

## Continuity

- Follows [*The Inner Light* (S05E25)](../episodes/225.md): Picard plays the Ressikan flute from his Kataan life with Nella Daren.
