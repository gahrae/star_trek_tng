## Synopsis

Scott, found alive in a transporter pattern buffer after seventy five years, feels useless aboard the Enterprise. When the ship is pulled inside a Dyson Sphere, he and La Forge use the wrecked Jenolen to hold the hatch open so it can escape. Picard gives him a shuttle.
