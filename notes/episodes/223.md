## Synopsis

A lone injured Borg, named Hugh by Geordi, develops individuality; Picard abandons a plan to use him to destroy the Collective, and Hugh chooses to return to the Borg.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Best Of Both Worlds, part 1* (S03E26)](../episodes/174.md): Picard, taken as Locutus in part 1, first refuses to treat Hugh as a person, then plays Locutus to test him.
- Leads to [*Descent, part 1* (S06E26)](../episodes/252.md): Hugh, sent back to the Borg in I, Borg, may explain why the Borg act as individuals; Picard defends the decision.
- Leads to [*Descent, part 2* (S07E01)](../episodes/253.md): Hugh, sent back to the Collective in I, Borg, says his individuality spread to other Borg.
