## Synopsis

After Klingon exchange officer J'Dan admits passing ship schematics to the Romulans, retired Admiral Satie widens the inquiry to Crewman Tarses, whose grandfather was Romulan. Picard calls it a drumhead trial and challenges her, and Admiral Henry ends the hearings after Satie attacks Picard.

## Log entries

> Captain's Log, Stardate 44769.2. For some weeks we have had a Klingon exobiologist on board as part of a scientific exchange programme. Unfortunately, we suspect that he was involved in a security breach and in the possible sabotage of our warp drive.

> Captain's log, supplemental. Retired Admiral Norah Satie, whose investigation exposed the alien conspiracy against Starfleet Command three years ago, is arriving to assist in our inquiry.

> Captain's log, supplemental. Admiral Thomas Henry, who has worked closely with Norah Satie in the past, has arrived to observe the hearings.
