## Synopsis

Answering an old distress beacon, the Enterprise rescues the Bringloidi farmers, then finds the Mariposan clones, who are failing from replicative fading and secretly take tissue from Riker and Pulaski. Picard brokers a deal in which the two colonies intermarry.
