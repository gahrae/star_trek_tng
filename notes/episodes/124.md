## Synopsis

Picard answers a distress call from scientist Paul Manheim, whose time experiments are distorting time across thousands of light years, and finds his wife Jenice, whom Picard once failed to meet in Paris. Data seals the breach, and Picard says goodbye to Jenice in a holodeck Paris.
