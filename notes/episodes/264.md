## Synopsis

Admiral Pressman brings the Enterprise to find the lost Pegasus before the Romulans do, secretly intending to restart its illegal phasing cloak experiment. Riker, who once backed him in a mutiny aboard the Pegasus, refuses, and Picard arrests Pressman for violating the Treaty of Algeron.

## Log entries

> Captain's log, stardate 47457.1. We have been trapped inside the asteroid for over eight hours. Mister Data and Commander La Forge inform me that they are nearly ready to engage the cloak.
