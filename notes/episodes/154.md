## Synopsis

After the Enterprise visits a dead Promellian cruiser, it is drained of power by hidden aceton assimilators. Geordi builds a holodeck Leah Brahms from computer records to help him, then proposes shutting almost everything off. Picard flies the ship out through the debris.

## Continuity

- Leads to [*Galaxy's Child* (S04E16)](../episodes/190.md): The real Leah Brahms discovers the holodeck version Geordi built in Booby Trap.
