## Synopsis

After a Nova Squadron cadet dies in a crash, squadron leader Locarno persuades Wesley and the others to hide that they attempted a banned Kolvoord Starburst. Picard works it out and tells Wesley to confess or he will. Wesley tells the inquiry; Locarno is expelled.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Final Mission* (S04E09)](../episodes/183.md): Wesley, told in Final Mission to report to the Academy, is a cadet facing an inquiry in The First Duty.
- Referenced by [*Lower Decks* (S07E15)](../episodes/267.md): Sito Jaxa, one of Wesley's Nova Squadron, is given a second chance by Picard.
