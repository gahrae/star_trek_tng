## Synopsis

Lwaxana Troi prepares to marry Minister Campio, whom she has never met, and befriends Alexander, undermining Worf's rules. Metal parasites eating the ship's nitrium nearly destroy it until Data lures them away with a nitrium beam. Lwaxana arrives at the wedding naked and Campio leaves.
