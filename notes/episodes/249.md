## Synopsis

Worf, in a crisis of faith, goes to Boreth, where Klingon Guardians present a returned Kahless. Gowron calls him an impostor, and Worf learns he is a clone. To avoid civil war, Worf proposes Kahless become Emperor as a figurehead, and Gowron accepts.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Redemption* (S04E26)](../episodes/200.md): Related rather than explicit: Gowron leads the High Council in both, with no reference back to the civil war.
