## Synopsis

Ambassador Odan, who is Crusher's lover, is revealed to be a symbiont when his host dies. Riker volunteers as temporary host so Odan can mediate a moon dispute, which he does, preventing war. Odan then moves to a new host, Kareel, and Crusher ends the relationship.

## Log entries

> Captain's log, stardate 44824.4. Governor Leka and the representatives of the two moons are ready to transport on board. We have learned that they each have troops massed and are ready for combat if this final effort at peace is not successful.
