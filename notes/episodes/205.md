## Synopsis

A quantum filament cripples the Enterprise. Troi, as senior bridge officer, resists Ro's push to separate the saucer, while Picard leads three schoolchildren up a damaged turboshaft and Worf delivers Keiko's baby. Riker and Data restore the failing containment field.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Data's Day* (S04E11)](../episodes/185.md): Keiko, married in Data's Day, gives birth with Worf's help.
