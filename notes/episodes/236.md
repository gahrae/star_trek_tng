## Synopsis

Vice Admiral Nechayev replaces Picard with Captain Jellico to negotiate with the Cardassians, while Picard, Worf and Crusher secretly enter Cardassian space to find a suspected metagenic weapon lab. It is a trap, and Picard is captured and interrogated by Madred.

## Continuity

- Follows [*The Wounded* (S04E12)](../episodes/186.md): Related rather than explicit: both are set on the Federation-Cardassian border after the peace treaty.
- Leads to [*Chain of Command, part 2* (S06E11)](../episodes/237.md): Resolves the cliffhanger: Picard's interrogation by Gul Madred.
