## Synopsis

Crusher watches people vanish from the ship, and everyone forgets them, until she is alone in a shrinking universe; she is trapped in a warp bubble from Wesley's experiment, and the returning Traveler helps Wesley open a way out.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Where No One Has Gone Before* (S01E06)](../episodes/106.md): The Traveler returns and helps Wesley open a gateway so his mother can leave the warp bubble.
