## Synopsis

Commander Maddox wants to dismantle Data to learn how to build more androids; at a hearing with Riker prosecuting and Picard defending, Captain Louvois rules that Data has the right to choose, and he refuses the procedure.

## Continuity

- Leads to [*The Offspring* (S03E16)](../episodes/164.md): Data's rights, upheld in his hearing, are cited by Picard when Admiral Haftel tries to take Data's daughter Lal.
- Leads to [*The Quality of Life* (S06E09)](../episodes/235.md): Data recalls Picard's defence of his own rights as the reason he stood up for the exocomps.
