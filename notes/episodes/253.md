## Synopsis

Lore controls Data and experiments on captured Enterprise officers while Crusher commands the ship, rescuing the stranded crew and destroying a Borg ship. Picard's team restores Data's ethical programme, Hugh turns on Lore, and Data shoots Lore to deactivate him.
