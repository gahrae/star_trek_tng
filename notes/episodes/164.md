## Synopsis

Data builds a daughter, Lal, and raises her aboard the Enterprise. Admiral Haftel orders her moved to Galor Four, and Picard backs Data's refusal. Lal feels fear, suffers cascade failure and dies; Data transfers her memories into himself.

## Continuity

- Follows [*The Measure of a Man* (S02E09)](../episodes/135.md): Data's rights, upheld in his hearing, are cited by Picard when Admiral Haftel tries to take Data's daughter Lal.

> Captain's log, supplemental. We are holding position pending the arrival of Admiral Haftel from Starfleet Research. Commander Data is completing his final neural transfers to the android he has named Lal, which I have learned, in the language Hindi means beloved.
