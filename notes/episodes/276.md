## Synopsis

Starfleet sends Lieutenant Ro undercover into the Maquis, and Picard has her lure them into a trap at the Hugora Nebula. After Macias is killed in a Cardassian attack, Ro warns the Maquis, abandons Starfleet and leaves with them, apologising to Picard.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Ensign Ro* (S05E03)](../episodes/203.md): Ro, brought aboard in Ensign Ro, goes undercover in the Maquis and leaves Starfleet to join them.
- Follows [*Journey's End* (S07E20)](../episodes/272.md): Related rather than explicit: both turn on the border treaty that leaves Federation colonists in Cardassian territory.
