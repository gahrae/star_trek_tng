## Synopsis

Dying K'mpec asks Picard to arbitrate the Klingon succession and find his poisoner. K'Ehleyr, who has arrived with Worf's son Alexander, investigates Duras over Khitomer and is killed; she names Duras, and Worf kills him despite Riker's arrival.

## Continuity

- Follows [*The Emissary* (S02E20)](../episodes/146.md): Worf and K'Ehleyr spent a night together in The Emissary; in Reunion she returns with their son Alexander.
- Follows [*Sins Of The Father* (S03E17)](../episodes/165.md): Duras, whose father Ja'rod was the real traitor, brought the charge against Mogh in Sins of the Father; Worf kills him in Reunion, though Mogh's innocence stays untold.
- Leads to [*Redemption* (S04E26)](../episodes/200.md): Picard, who arbitrated the succession in Reunion, is told he is 'already involved' when Duras' family challenges Gowron.
- Referenced by [*New Ground* (S05E10)](../episodes/210.md): Alexander, who lost his mother in Reunion, is brought to live with Worf on the Enterprise.
