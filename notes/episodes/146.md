## Synopsis

Federation emissary K'Ehleyr, whom Worf knew six years earlier, joins the Enterprise to stop a Klingon ship whose crew, asleep for seventy-five years, will attack Federation colonies believing the war continues. Worf's bluff as commander forces Captain K'Temoc to yield. K'Ehleyr and Worf part with feelings unresolved.

## Continuity

- Leads to [*Reunion* (S04E07)](../episodes/181.md): Worf and K'Ehleyr spent a night together in The Emissary; in Reunion she returns with their son Alexander.

## Cast

From Memory Alpha. Linked where the credit could be matched to a transcript label.

**Cast**

- Suzie Plakson was cast as K'Ehleyr because the production staff were impressed with her performance as Doctor Selar in " The Schizoid Man ". Tracy Tormé , who had written "The Schizoid Man", had wanted to develop a romance between Selar and Worf, but the idea was overruled when "The Emissary" was being planned. Tormé complained that the premise was " obvious. Had it been a Vulcan, it would have been a lot more interesting. " ( Captains' Logs: The Unauthorized Complete Trek Voyages , p. 182) Plakson recalled, " There was a line – 'Sorry, I had to make myself beautiful' – and I thought this was riotous, because I had this big old wrinkly forehead on. Anyway, it's not like I played it for huge laughs, but I've done a lot of parlor comedies and that was the kind of attitude I gave it. The people who were listening to me didn't bat an eyelash, but after I got the role, someone came up to me and said, 'Suzie, there is no winking in the twenty-fourth century. " ( Star Trek: The Next Generation 365 , p. 112) ([K'Ehleyr](../characters/k-ehleyr.md))
