## Synopsis

Worf's mother leaves his son Alexander with him aboard the Enterprise. Alexander lies and steals, and Worf plans to send him to a Klingon school. After Alexander is hurt in a fire during a rescue, Worf lets him stay with him.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Reunion* (S04E07)](../episodes/181.md): Alexander, who lost his mother in Reunion, is brought to live with Worf on the Enterprise.
- Leads to [*Firstborn* (S07E21)](../episodes/273.md): Worf's worry in New Ground that Alexander will not live as a Klingon returns when Alexander, as K'mtar, comes back from the future.

## Log entries
