## Synopsis

Picard has a faulty artificial heart replaced at a starbase and Pulaski helps save him when the surgery goes wrong; meanwhile Riker outwits the slow-witted but dangerous Pakleds, who have taken Geordi hostage.

## Continuity

- Leads to [*Tapestry* (S06E15)](../episodes/241.md): Q shows Picard the bar fight that cost him his heart, mentioned in Samaritan Snare.
