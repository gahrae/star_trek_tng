## Synopsis

Gul Madred tortures the captured Picard for Starfleet's defence plans, demanding he say he sees five lights. Meanwhile Jellico, with Riker piloting, mines the Cardassian ships hidden in a nebula and forces them to withdraw and release Picard, who says there are four lights.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Chain of Command, part 1* (S06E10)](../episodes/236.md): Resolves the cliffhanger: Picard's interrogation by Gul Madred.
