## Synopsis

Worf's foster brother Nikolai smuggles a village of Boraalans onto the holodeck to save them from their planet's death; Picard reluctantly helps relocate them, and Nikolai stays with the villagers.

## Log entries

> Captain's log, stardate 47423.9. We have arrived at Boraal Two in response to an emergency distress call from Lieutenant Worf's foster brother, Nikolai Rozhenko. He has been stationed on the planet as a cultural observer.

> Captain's log, stardate 47427.2. The Boraalans have safely reached the site of what will eventually become their new village. None of them suspects they ever left their planet. However, our success has come at a high price.
