## Synopsis

Worf, thrown between quantum realities after his shuttle crosses a fissure, finds himself married to Troi in one of them. As more Enterprises flood into Riker's universe, Worf takes the original shuttle back through the fissure, reseals it and returns home.

## Continuity

- Leads to [*Eye of the Beholder* (S07E18)](../episodes/270.md): Related rather than explicit: Worf and Troi are married in one reality in Parallels and become lovers in Eye of the Beholder.
