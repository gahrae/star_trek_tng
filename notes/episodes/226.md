## Synopsis

Data's head is found in a cave beneath San Francisco, dated five centuries old. While the Enterprise investigates aliens on Devidia, Data is pulled into 1893 San Francisco, where he finds Guinan and Clemens. Picard's away team follows the aliens through a portal.

## Continuity

- Leads to [*Time's Arrow, part 2* (S06E01)](../episodes/227.md): Resolves the cliffhanger: Picard's team follows the aliens through their portal to nineteenth-century San Francisco.
