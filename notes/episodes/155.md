## Synopsis

Geordi is stranded on storm-wracked Galorndon Core with a Romulan and they must cooperate to survive; Worf refuses to give the ribosomes a dying Romulan needs, and the Enterprise faces down a Romulan warbird at the border.

## Continuity

- Leads to [*The Defector* (S03E10)](../episodes/158.md): Tomalak, met over Galorndon Core, returns as the architect of the defector trap.

## Log entries

We have recovered one survivor, but Lieutenant Commander La Forge did not report back with the away team and is still missing.
