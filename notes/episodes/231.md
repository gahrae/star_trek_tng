## Synopsis

Crew members, Riker among them, are taken from their beds by aliens from a tertiary subspace domain, drawn in by La Forge's sensor modification. Riker carries a homing device to the alien lab so the Enterprise can close the rupture with a graviton pulse, and he escapes with Ensign Rager.
