## Synopsis

Lwaxana Troi, in a Betazoid mid-life phase, pursues Picard, who hides in his Dixon Hill holodeck programme. She turns to Riker, then to the holodeck bartender Rex. She finally reads the minds of two Antedean delegates and exposes them as assassins carrying explosives.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Haven* (S01E11)](../episodes/105.md): Lwaxana returns, still pursuing a match, this time for herself; Mr Homn is back as her valet.
- Follows [*The Big Goodbye* (S01E12)](../episodes/113.md): Picard retreats into the Dixon Hill holonovel to escape Lwaxana.
