## Synopsis

DaiMon Tog of the Ferengi kidnaps Lwaxana Troi for her telepathy, along with Deanna and Riker. Riker hides a signal in the static, and Lwaxana offers to stay with Tog. Picard's staged jealous ultimatum gets her back. Wesley misses the Academy exam and is promoted to full Ensign.

## Log entries

> Captain's log, stardate 43930.7. The Enterprise has been in attendance at the biennial Trade Agreements Conference on Betazed. For the first time, the Ferengi are present, and I have reluctantly consented to their boarding the Enterprise for the closing reception.
