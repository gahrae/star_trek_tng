## Synopsis

Picard recovers from his Borg ordeal at the family vineyard in France, fights it out with his brother Robert, and turns down the Atlantis project; Worf's parents visit, and Wesley watches a message from his late father.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Best Of Both Worlds, part 1* (S03E26)](../episodes/174.md): Picard recovers from assimilation at his family vineyard.
- Follows [*Best of Both Worlds, part 2* (S04E01)](../episodes/175.md): The Enterprise is under refit after the Borg incident and Picard recovers at home in France.

## Log entries

> Captain's Log: Stardate 44012.3 The Enterprise remains docked at McKinley Station, undergoing a major overhaul and refit following the Borg incident. I am confident that the ship and her crew will soon be ready to return to service.
