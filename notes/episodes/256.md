## Synopsis

Believing Picard was killed, Riker investigates and is captured by Baran's mercenaries, who are looting Romulan artefacts. He finds Picard alive, undercover as "Galen", and agrees to play a disgraced officer to win Baran's trust. Riker shuts down the Enterprise's shields, and the mercenaries fire on it.

## Continuity

- Leads to [*Gambit, part 2* (S07E05)](../episodes/257.md): Resolves the cliffhanger: the artefacts Baran's mercenaries loot are Vulcan pieces of the Stone of Gol, a psionic weapon.
