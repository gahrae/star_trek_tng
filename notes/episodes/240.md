## Synopsis

Troi wakes surgically altered as Tal Shiar Major Rakal aboard a Romulan warbird. Subcommander N'Vek, a dissident, needs her to deliver defecting Vice Proconsul M'ret to the Federation. She bluffs Commander Toreth, then tricks the Enterprise into lowering shields and transports M'ret across. N'Vek dies and Troi is rescued.
