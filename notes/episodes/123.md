## Synopsis

The Enterprise rescues Ornaran and Brekkian traders over a cargo of felicium, which Dr. Crusher finds is an addictive narcotic, not a medicine. Citing the Prime Directive, Picard will not intervene, lets the shipment go and withholds the freighter repair coils.
