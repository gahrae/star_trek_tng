## Synopsis

Troi loses her empathic sense and tries to resign as counsellor. Meanwhile the Enterprise is towed by two-dimensional beings toward a cosmic string fragment. Troi suggests they are drawn to it by instinct, a simulated string frees the ship, and her sense returns as the beings reach it.

## Log entries
