## Synopsis

Lieutenant Barclay, mocked by colleagues as 'Broccoli', escapes into holodeck fantasies of the crew while a spreading malfunction caused by invidium contamination threatens the ship; his insight helps solve it.

## Continuity

- Leads to [*The Nth Degree* (S04E19)](../episodes/193.md): Barclay, who used to retreat into the holodeck, has his intelligence boosted by an alien probe.
