## Synopsis

Data, memoryless after a power surge, lives in a pre-industrial village and is blamed for a sickness caused by radioactive probe fragments. He cures it, is killed by the villagers, and is recovered by the Enterprise. Meanwhile Troi passes Riker's Bridge Officer's test and is promoted.
