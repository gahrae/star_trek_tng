## Synopsis

Geordi, who has a fantasy about a holographic Leah Brahms, meets the real, hostile Dr Brahms. Meanwhile the Enterprise kills a space creature, delivers its baby, and the two engineers wean the baby off the ship's power. Leah finds the hologram, they quarrel, then reconcile.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Booby Trap* (S03E06)](../episodes/154.md): The real Leah Brahms discovers the holodeck version Geordi built in Booby Trap.
