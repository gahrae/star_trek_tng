## Synopsis

Geordi studies the logs of missing Lieutenant Aquiel Uhnari and falls for her when Klingons return her alive. Crusher finds the remains on the relay station are a coalescent organism that copies what it absorbs. Aquiel and Morag are held for observation, and Geordi destroys the creature when it copies his dog.

## Log entries
