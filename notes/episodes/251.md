## Synopsis

Returning from a conference, Picard, Troi, Data and Geordi find the Enterprise and a Romulan warbird frozen in fragmented time, with a warp core breach slowly expanding. They learn aliens nesting in the Romulan engine core caused it, reverse time, and Picard breaks the power transfer, saving the ship.
