## Synopsis

Data narrates a day in his life to Commander Maddox: he learns to dance, his friendly meddling nearly derails the wedding of O'Brien and Keiko, and he helps uncover a Romulan spy posing as a Vulcan ambassador.

## Continuity

- Leads to [*Disaster* (S05E05)](../episodes/205.md): Keiko, married in Data's Day, gives birth with Worf's help.

## Log entries

> Second officer's personal log, supplemental. The risks aboard a starship are accepted by all who serve, but I have never failed to observe a deep emotional response to the loss of a comrade. It is at times like this that I greatly miss the ability to share human feelings.
