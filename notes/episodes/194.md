## Synopsis

Q, saying he is repaying a debt, throws Picard and his officers into a Robin Hood story to prove that loving Vash makes him weak. Picard rescues Vash as Maid Marian. Afterward she leaves with Q as his new partner.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Captain's Holiday* (S03E19)](../episodes/167.md): Vash, met on Risa, comes aboard and leaves with Q.
