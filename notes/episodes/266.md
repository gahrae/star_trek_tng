## Synopsis

Crusher inherits her grandmother's house on Caldos and is seduced by Ronin, an anaphasic entity that has lived with her family's women for generations; he kills to protect himself, and she destroys him.

## Log entries

> Captain's log, supplemental. Commander La Forge and Mister Data have recovered from their exposure to the anaphasic energy, but I'm afraid Doctor Crusher's recovery will be of a more personal nature.
