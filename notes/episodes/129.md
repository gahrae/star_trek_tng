## Synopsis

Geordi asks the computer for an opponent capable of defeating Data, and Professor Moriarty becomes self-aware, takes over the holodeck and holds Pulaski. He wants to keep existing; Picard cannot free him, so Moriarty returns control and his programme is saved.

## Continuity

- Leads to [*Ship in a Bottle* (S06E12)](../episodes/238.md): Moriarty, left stored in the computer at the end of Elementary, Dear Data, is reactivated and says Picard has not kept his promise to free him.
