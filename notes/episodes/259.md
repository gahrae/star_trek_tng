## Synopsis

Lwaxana collapses after telepathic work with the Cairn. With Maques as a telepathic bridge, Troi enters her mother's metaconscious and finds Lwaxana's grief over the drowning of Troi's older sister Kestra. Troi persuades her to let go, and Lwaxana wakes.

## Log entries

> Captain's log, stardate 47254.1. A delegation of the Cairn have just come on board. This telepathic species has no concept of spoken language, and is being instructed in its use by an old friend.
