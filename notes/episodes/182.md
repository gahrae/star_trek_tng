## Synopsis

Riker wakes sixteen years in the future as captain with a son, then spots flaws and a false Romulan plot to extract Outpost twenty three's location. That too proves illusory: a lonely alien boy, Barash, made it all, wanting someone real to stay. Riker offers to take him along.

## Continuity

- Follows [*11001001* (S01E15)](../episodes/116.md): Minuet, the Bynars' holodeck woman, reappears as Riker's 'wife', exposing the false future.

## Log entries

> Captain's Log, Stardate 44286.5. The Enterprise is conducting a security survey of the Onias Sector near the Neutral Zone. Despite our proximity to Romulan territory, the mission has been quiet and uneventful.
