## Synopsis

Geordi and his former colleague Susanna Leijten investigate why members of their old Tarchannen III away team vanish. Susanna and then Geordi transform into alien creatures. Geordi flees to the planet, and Susanna leads the away team to him. Surgery restores him.

## Log entries
