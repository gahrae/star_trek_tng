## Synopsis

Iyaaran ambassadors visit the Enterprise to experience pleasure, antagonism and love. Troi hosts chocolate-loving Loquel and Worf endures the rude Byleth, while Picard, stranded on a planet, is courted by Anna, who turns out to be Voval in disguise.
