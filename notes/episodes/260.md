## Synopsis

Prytt captors implant Picard and Crusher on Kesprytt III so they can read their minds; the implants link the two telepathically as they escape, and Picard's long-hidden feelings for her come out, while Riker works the Kes-Prytt dispute for their release.

## Log entries

> Captain's log, stardate 47304.2. The Enterprise has arrived at Kesprytt Three in order to evaluate an unusual request on the part of the Kes for associate membership in the Federation.
