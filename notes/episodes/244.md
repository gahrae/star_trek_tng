## Synopsis

During a baryon sweep of the evacuated Enterprise, Picard fights Kelsey's team, who are stealing trilithium resin, while Riker, Data and Crusher are held hostage at Arkaria base by Orton. Kelsey escapes with the container, but the thieves' ship is destroyed.

## Log entries

> Captain's log, stardate 46682.4. The Enterprise is docked at the Remmler Array, where it will undergo a routine procedure to eliminate accumulated baryon particles. In preparation for the sweep, we are evacuating the ship.
