## Synopsis

Picard brings Sovereign Marouk to offer amnesty to the Gatherers. Riker befriends her servant Yuta, but finds she is the last of the Tralesta clan, altered to live long and kill the Lornak with an engineered virus. He cannot stop her and vapourises her.

## Log entries

> Captain's log, Stardate 43421.9. In an effort to put an end to the Gatherer raids, we have come to the Acamar System to enlist the aid of Marouk, the Sovereign of Acamar Three.
