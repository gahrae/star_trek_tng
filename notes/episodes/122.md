## Synopsis

On Vagra II, the oil-like Armus kills Tasha Yar for amusement and holds Troi and Lieutenant Prieto in their crashed shuttle, also seizing Riker. Picard refuses to bargain, Armus's guard drops, and Troi and Prieto are beamed up. Tasha is mourned in a holodeck farewell.

## Continuity

- Leads to [*Yesterday's Enterprise* (S03E15)](../episodes/163.md): The alternate timeline undoes Tasha's pointless death and gives her a meaningful one.
- Leads to [*Legacy* (S04E06)](../episodes/180.md): Tasha's sister Ishara appears, and Data tells her Tasha was killed on Vagra Two.

> Captain's log, supplemental. There is grave danger to the crew on Vagra Two. My first officer is missing, attacked by this entity known as Armus.

> Captain's log, stardate 41602.1. The shuttlecraft has been destroyed to prevent any possibility of Armus leaving the planet. Vagra Two will be declared off limits. But the damage has been done. One of the saddest duties I've ever had to perform is now ahead of me.
