## Synopsis

Barzan's Premier Bhavani takes bids for a stable wormhole aboard the Enterprise. Negotiator Devinoni Ral, who is part Betazoid, romances Troi and uses empathy to win for the Chrysalians, staging a Ferengi missile threat. Troi exposes him. Geordi and Data find the wormhole worthless.

## Log entries

> Captain's log, Stardate 43385.6. We are orbiting Barzan Two, which is entertaining bids for control of what appears to be a stable wormhole, which could provide a permanent shortcut to the distant Gamma Quadrant.
