## Synopsis

The Enterprise revives three humans frozen since the 20th century and makes first contact in decades with the Romulans, who say they have also lost outposts to an unknown enemy; Federation outposts have been destroyed too.

## Continuity

- Leads to [*Q Who?* (S02E16)](../episodes/142.md): Outposts along the Neutral Zone were carried off in The Neutral Zone; in Q Who? Data compares a stripped planet to them just before the Borg cube appears.

## Log entries
