## Synopsis

Bok, stripped of his DaiMon rank, returns to avenge his son's death at Picard's hands; he threatens Picard's supposed son Jason Vigo, whose DNA he has faked, until Picard sees through it and confronts him on his ship.

## Continuity

- Follows [*The Battle* (S01E09)](../episodes/110.md): Bok, whose son died when Picard destroyed his ship, returns for revenge.
