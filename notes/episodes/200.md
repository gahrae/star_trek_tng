## Synopsis

Gowron's succession is challenged by Toral, son of Duras, and his aunts Lursa and B'Etor, who have Romulan allies; Picard refuses to take sides, and Worf resigns from Starfleet to serve Gowron, who restores his honour.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Sins Of The Father* (S03E17)](../episodes/165.md): Worf, discommended in Sins of the Father, has his honour restored by Gowron.
- Follows [*Reunion* (S04E07)](../episodes/181.md): Picard, who arbitrated the succession in Reunion, is told he is 'already involved' when Duras' family challenges Gowron.
- Leads to [*Redemption part 2* (S05E01)](../episodes/201.md): Resolves the cliffhanger: Sela's Romulan support for the Duras family is exposed.
- Referenced by [*Yesterday's Enterprise* (S03E15)](../episodes/163.md)
- Referenced by [*The Mind's Eye* (S04E24)](../episodes/198.md)
- Referenced by [*Unification, part 2* (S05E08)](../episodes/207.md): Related rather than explicit: a Romulan woman backs the Duras sisters in Redemption; in Unification the Romulan Sela runs the plot against Vulcan.
- Referenced by [*Rightful Heir* (S06E23)](../episodes/249.md): Related rather than explicit: Gowron leads the High Council in both, with no reference back to the civil war.
