## Synopsis

Wesley returns from the Academy troubled and resigns after a vision of his father; the Federation hands the colony world Dorvan V to Cardassia, its colonists choose to stay under Cardassian rule, and the Traveler reveals himself and offers to guide Wesley.

## Continuity

- Follows [*Where No One Has Gone Before* (S01E06)](../episodes/106.md): The Traveler returns as Lakanta and offers to guide Wesley, who stays on Dorvan V to study.
- Leads to [*Preemptive Strike* (S07E24)](../episodes/276.md): Related rather than explicit: both turn on the border treaty that leaves Federation colonists in Cardassian territory.
