## Synopsis

Riker volunteers for an exchange posting as first officer of the Klingon ship Pagh. When a hull-eating organism appears, Captain Kargan blames the Enterprise and prepares to attack. Riker keeps his oaths, has Kargan beamed away, takes command briefly, then hands it back.

## Log entries

> Captain's log, stardate 42506.5. We have departed from Starbase one seven nine and are headed for a rendezvous with the Klingon vessel, Pagh. I have informed the staff of Commander Riker's temporary assignment.

> First Officer's personal log. I have aboard the Pagh a short time, but long enough to be impressed with the abilities and single-mindedness of the Klingons.

> Captain's log, Stardate 42507.8. Although our search has been extensive, we are still unable to locate the Klingon ship or any evidence of it's destruction.
