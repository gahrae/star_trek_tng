## Synopsis

Captain Maxwell of the Phoenix, who lost his family in a Cardassian raid, destroys Cardassian ships, claiming they are rearming. Picard, with Gul Macet aboard, tracks him down and, with O'Brien's help, persuades him to surrender, preserving the peace and warning Macet that the Federation is watching.
