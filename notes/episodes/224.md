## Synopsis

After a transporter accident, La Forge and Ro are phased by a Romulan experimental device and believed dead. Unseen, they attend their own memorial party and uncover the Romulans' plan to wreck the Enterprise's warp engines. Data's anyon beam makes them visible again.
