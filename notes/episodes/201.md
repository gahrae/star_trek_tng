## Synopsis

Picard sets a tachyon detection blockade on the Romulan-Klingon border to expose Romulan help for the Duras. Data, commanding the Sutherland, disobeys orders and fires on the hidden convoy, so Sela withdraws. Gowron wins, and Worf spares the captured Toral.
